Galvanized · Polybutylene · Lead lines

Whole-home repiping

Low pressure and rusty water usually mean pipes past their life. We repipe in copper or PEX through small, planned openings, then patch the drywall ourselves. Most homes take two to four days, and you have water every night.

Camera · Hydro-jetting · Trenchless

Questions we hear every week

Do you charge extra for nights and weekends?

Emergency calls after 6 pm, on weekends and on holidays carry a $75 after-hours fee. The flat rate for the repair itself does not change.

What if the job takes longer than you planned?

You pay the price you approved. Our rates are per job, not per hour, so a stubborn fitting costs us time, not you money.

Are your plumbers employees or subcontractors?

Every plumber is a full-time employee who passed a background check and drug screen. Apprentices always work beside a licensed journeyman.

Do you offer financing?

Yes, for jobs over $1,000, with 12 months same-as-cash on approved credit. Ask your plumber for the application on site.
